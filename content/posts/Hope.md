Somewhere,
Out there,
There is a place,
And a time

Eternal light,
Eternal hope,
Internal light?
Burned out.

Forever hope,
It shines Eternal,
Shines bright in the sky
Humanity makes it glow

Bright future,
After the darkest of nights,
For how long?
Too long.

Alfred Newman
