Liberation.
Freedom.
I always imagine myself…
on the top of a mountain,

at the top of a cliff,
or floating on nothing
with a white wide world.
Alone at last

Looking through the valley below,
The sun shines forever…
The light cuts through my eyes,
it blinds me… 

I feel…
Accomplished.
Is it the end..?
Of course… 

It is never a good ending,
no matter how cinematic…
no matter how exciting…
no matter how…

Happy..?
It always is,
and always will be...
Forever a bad ending… 

As the sun shines bright in the blue skies…
It fills me with…
Happiness..?
Accomplishment.

I am finally,
truly…
Free…
Am I..?

The bright light delivers…
Hope.
I have Hope…
Do I?

Alfred Newman
