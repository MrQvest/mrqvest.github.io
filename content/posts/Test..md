the darkest place one can go
the deepest rabbit Hole
the nine are waiting for me,
the journey towards the center.

it feels like Eternity,
the war machine rages on,
more And more lost souls wander,
through the Vivid empty battlefield

the End is near,
though it is no bad ending,
as the New world becomes clear,
and hope Shines eternal.

Alfred Newman
