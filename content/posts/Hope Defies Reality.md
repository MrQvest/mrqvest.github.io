As time moves forward,
I start to realize
life is as meaningless as you make it.
As I write these letters,

that later form into words,
as beautiful as the mind allows it,
and as free as the universe’s mysteries…
I realize,

the only certainty is the uncertainty…
is the mind real..?
is the sense of self real..?
how truly real is the universe..?

reality is merely one building block,
only a part of what makes the mind…
in fact,
the mind makes reality.

And if I choose what to make of my reality…
I choose hope.
As hope dies last,
and Hope Shines Eternal…

Alfred Newman
